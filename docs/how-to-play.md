# How to Play Zot-Life: Survive the Week at UCI 🎮

## Objective
Make it through a week at UCI without burning out. Choose wisely, every decision affects your energy!

## How to Play
- Click one of the two buttons for each scenario.
- Each choice affects your energy.
- Game ends when your energy drops to 0.

## Tips
- Running everywhere isn't always smart.
- Food helps, but it wastes time!
